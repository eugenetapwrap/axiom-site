## What would flip a no

- If the free 30 come back right on our worst handwritten notas and blurry photos, with no fixing from me, I'd pay without thinking about it.
- A full free month, so I can see one whole month of the café in the sheet with my admin not typing anything, before I put in a card.
- If it caught the receipts my site manager pays in cash that never show up in my bank, and compared them against the build budget so I could see overspend before I send the next transfer.
- Running my own pile of real handwritten nota through the free 30 and seeing almost all of them come out right with no fixing. If it handles those better than what I could hack together, $19 is fine.
- If it pushed the receipts straight into Zoho or Jurnal instead of a separate sheet, so my bookkeeper keeps her setup and I only use it to grab receipts from the Telegram groups.
- If during the free 30 it actually flags a duplicate or a padded total from my scooter repairs that I missed myself, I'd pay the $19 straight away.
- Another villa operator in Bali I know telling me they've used it for months and it caught a duplicate or a padded receipt for them.
- If I could just forward receipts to it myself without adding it to any group or connecting my Google account, and get the sheet back as a file, I would try the free 30.
- If my admin ran the free 30 documents on a messy week of fuel and parts receipts and told me it saved her most of a day without errors, I'd pay.
- Seeing it on my own receipts during the free 30: a scribbled mechanic note and a fuel slip turned into correct rows in my sheet, plus proof it caught a duplicate or a wrong total my admin missed.
- Another contractor here in Bali I know telling me they've run it on their site chats for a few months with no problems, plus a real company name and a clear statement of what it stores and deletes.
- My bookkeeper telling me she already uses it with her other clients and wants me on it, or another contractor I know who has run it for six months or more.
