Nota (@NotaGetBot) — forward a receipt and forget about it: a minute later it is a row in your expense sheet.
Open the bot and send any receipt, invoice, transfer screenshot or handwritten note. Nota replies with what it read and warns you if it is a duplicate. The bot sees only the one sheet and folder it creates.
