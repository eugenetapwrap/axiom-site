Nota (@NotaGetBot) — a Telegram bot that shows where your business money went.
Export your work chat with receipts (Telegram or WhatsApp) and send the archive to the bot. In about 25 minutes you get a spending report: totals by category, duplicate payments and errors in amounts. Every document also becomes a row in your own Google Sheet with a link to the original.
After that, forward new receipts or add the bot to the work chat; staff change nothing. The sheet updates itself and you get a weekly report.
First 30 documents free, no card, no sign-up. Then $19/month for 100 documents, $35 for 200, $79 for 500.
