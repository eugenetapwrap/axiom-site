## What would flip a no

- Seeing it read my worst real receipts during the free 30, the handwritten and faded ones from the group, with the right totals and catching a duplicate I had missed.
- If after the free documents my staff are still sending receipts to the bot on their own and my admin tells me the sheet matches what she would have typed, then I pay for the 200 plan.
- If during the free 30 it reads a pile of my own messy Indonesian nota without mistakes and flags a real double-claim or a padded total I would have missed, then I'd pay the $35 without thinking twice.
- If it showed me on the first free batch that my site manager's receipts don't add up to what I wired, and it cost under $10 a month or a one-off fee per build.
- If my staff's actual handwritten nota from the free 30 come back with the right totals and item names nearly every time, with only a few rows to fix, I'd pay rather than build and maintain my own bot.
- If it could push straight into the app my bookkeeper already uses, so the Telegram receipts stop getting lost without changing how we keep the books, and I could see a real company behind it with other Bali contractors using it for a year.
- Seeing it run in our actual work group through one full month-end, so my accountant gets a complete set without me chasing anybody.
- If during the free 30 it flags a real duplicate or a bad total from my scooter repairs that I missed myself, I'd pay the $19.
- Another villa operator in Bali telling me they've used it for six months with their staff in the group and it hasn't missed receipts or broken. That, plus seeing it catch a real duplicate or wrong total in my own groups during the free trial.
- A way to get the sheet without connecting Google at all, like a file it sends me every week, plus a clear name and company behind it that I can look up.
- If my admin tried the free 30 on a week of scooter parts and fuel and told me it cut her Excel day down to almost nothing, I'd pay without thinking about it.
- If the free 30 documents read my mechanics' handwritten notes and fuel slips correctly and my admin could stop the weekly typing, I'd pay the $35 tier.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
