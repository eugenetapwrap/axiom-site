Nota (@NotaGetBot) — a Telegram bot that collects your clients' receipts for you.
Add it to the Telegram chat where a client's staff send receipts, or forward documents to it privately. The client's staff change nothing and install nothing.
It reads receipts, invoices, PDFs, bank-transfer screenshots and handwritten nota in any language, checks line items against the total, catches duplicates even when a receipt is re-photographed or sent again by someone else, and writes each document as a row in a Google Sheet with a link to the original. Every Monday it sends a weekly spending report.
Setup takes one minute (sign in with Google; the sheet and files stay in that Google Drive). Up to 5 groups per account, each group becomes a separate business unit in the sheet. No direct export to Jurnal or Accurate yet; the sheet can be copied or downloaded.
First 40 documents free. Then $19/month for 100 documents, $45 for 300, $119 for 1,000.
