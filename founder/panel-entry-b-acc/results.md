## What would flip a no

- A one-click export or import file that goes straight into Jurnal or Accurate, mapped to my clients' accounts, so the receipts end up in the books without me re-keying them.
- A bookkeeper plan where each client's chat goes into that client's own sheet automatically, priced so I can pass the cost on to clients, like a few dollars per client a month.
- Run one real client's full month through it next to my own entries and have it come out matching, so I only need to spot-check rather than re-check every line, with a multi-client setup priced per firm instead of per document.
- A way to pass the cost to each client, like a small plan per client they pay themselves, or a cheap bookkeeper plan for around $10 a month covering all my clients.
- If it worked inside the WhatsApp chats I already have, put each client into their own sheet, and nagged clients about missing receipts before month end.
- A clear statement of where documents are stored and who can see them, UU PDP compliant, plus a separate sheet per client chat under one account priced for a solo bookkeeper.
- A direct export to Jurnal or Accurate with invoice number, PPN and NPWP pulled out of the receipt, at a bookkeeper price I can pass on to my clients, around $15 a month for enough documents to cover all of them.
- A direct export to Jurnal or Accurate with invoice number, PPN and NPWP pulled out, plus one firm account that covers all my client chats at a reseller price.
- If I could use it privately on my side, one sheet per client and no bot in their chats, at a price I could pass on to clients or under $10 a month.
- A direct export or ready-made import file for Jurnal and Accurate, so I can forward the receipts myself without adding the bot to my clients' chats.
