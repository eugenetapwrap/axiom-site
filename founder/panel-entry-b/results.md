## What would flip a no

- Seeing it get a week of my own staff's worst receipts right during the free 30, handwritten ones included, without me touching anything.
- If after the free documents my admin tells me she did not have to type anything on Monday and the staff kept sending without me reminding them, then $19 is nothing.
- Catching a real overcharge or double payment on my build during the free trial, one big enough to cover a year of the fee, and then paying per document only in the months I actually use it.
- If the free 30 documents come back from my staff's real handwritten nota with the totals right and almost nothing for me to fix.
- If it pushed the receipts from our Telegram groups straight into Zoho or Jurnal instead of a separate Google Sheet, and there was a real company with a track record behind it, I'd try it on one site.
- Another villa operator I know in Bali using it for a few months, telling me the totals match and it caught a duplicate, plus a real company name I can look up.
- If it worked without touching my Google account or joining the group chat, like I forward receipts myself and it gives me back a spreadsheet file, and a real local company I can find stands behind it.
- My admin runs the free 30 on a real week of our scooter parts and fuel receipts, it gets them right without fixing, and it saves her hours so she can stop doing the Friday catch-up.
- If the free 30 documents showed it reading the handwritten mechanic notes and fuel receipts correctly, and my admin actually stopped being a week behind without extra work from me.
- A real company I can check, plus a clear guarantee that the bot only gets access to one sheet it creates, not my whole Google account, and only reads receipts in the chat, not our conversations. Better still if another Bali contractor I know is already using it.
- My bookkeeper telling me she wants it and will set it up herself, ideally with another contractor I know saying they've used it for months without problems.
- If during the free part my staff kept sending receipts without me chasing them, and it caught a duplicate or a wrong total that saved me real money, I would pay the $19.
