## What would flip a no

- A direct import into Jurnal or Accurate, so the receipts end up in my books without retyping.
- A clear data processing agreement that complies with UU PDP, plus PPN, NPWP and invoice number fields and a direct import file for Accurate or Jurnal, proven on one of my real clients' month of nota.
- An accountant plan with unlimited client groups, each one in its own sheet, plus a clear written data-processing statement covering UU PDP (who sees the images, where they're stored, deletion on request).
- If it worked inside the WhatsApp groups my clients already use, and there was a reseller price so I could add it as a paid extra and keep a margin.
- A plan around $5 a month for a solo bookkeeper with a few clients, plus proof that clients are fine with the bot in their chats, like a short message I could send them explaining what it sees and keeps.
- Seeing it run a full month on one of my real clients with almost no corrections, plus enough groups to cover all my clients so I can take on more of them.
- If it worked inside WhatsApp, where my clients already send receipts, I'd try the 40 free documents on my messiest client.
- A direct import into Jurnal or Accurate, at a price per account under about $10 a month that covers all my clients, not per client.
- If it worked inside the WhatsApp groups my clients already use, and exported straight into Accurate or Jurnal so I could take on a few more clients without hiring.
- If it worked in WhatsApp groups too and gave me a file I could import straight into Jurnal or Accurate.
- An accountant plan: one login, 20+ clients each with a separate sheet, an import file Jurnal or Accurate accepts, and a reseller or volume price so I can bill it on top of my bookkeeping fee.
- A direct import file for Jurnal and Accurate, so the rows go straight into my accounting system without retyping.
