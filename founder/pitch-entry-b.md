Nota (@NotaGetBot) — expense tracking that starts in one minute.
Open the bot, forward any receipt, invoice, bank-transfer screenshot or handwritten note, and connect your Google account. About a minute later the document is a row in your own Google Sheet with a link to the original.
Add the bot to the work chat and staff keep sending receipts as usual. Nota checks the totals, flags duplicates and sends a weekly report.
First 30 documents free, no card, no sign-up. Then $19/month for 100 documents, $35 for 200, $79 for 500.
