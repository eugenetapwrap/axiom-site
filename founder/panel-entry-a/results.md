## What would flip a no

- Seeing it read a month of our own messy handwritten notas from the villa group with almost no mistakes in the free trial, and flag a real duplicate payment I had missed.
- A full first month free, so my admin can stop typing receipts for four weeks and I actually get the weekly report on my phone without anyone chasing it.
- If the free 30 reads my actual handwritten nota without mistakes and flags something real I missed, like the same material bought at very different prices or a double claim, I'd pay $19 the same day.
- If the free report actually caught a real overcharge or double payment on my build that I had missed, I would pay for the months the build is running and cancel afterwards.
- If the free 30 documents, run on my own real handwritten nota, came back with the amounts and items right with almost nothing to correct.
- If it plugged straight into Jurnal or Zoho so it fed my current setup instead of replacing it, and the free run actually found real duplicate payments or wrong amounts in my site chats.
- If the free run on my own chat actually caught a duplicate payment or a wrong amount, or gave my accountant the month-end documents in a form she accepts without chasing me, I'd pay.
- Another villa owner I know telling me they've used it for months and it caught real duplicate payments, so I'd know it's legit and sticking around.
- A version that works without my Google account and without joining the group, where I forward only the receipts I pick, from a known local company that deletes everything after processing and says so in writing.
- If the free run on my own chat turned up real money I was losing, like a part paid for twice or a fuel receipt with the wrong amount, and it covered more than the $19 a month.
- If the free run on my own chat found duplicate or inflated payments worth more than a few months of the subscription, like a part paid twice or a fuel amount that doesn't match, I'd pay.
- Another builder I know here using it for a few months and telling me it's fine, and a way to try it by forwarding one batch of receipts without adding it to the group or linking my Google account.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
