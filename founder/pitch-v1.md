Nota (@NotaGetBot) — a Telegram bot that keeps your business expenses under control.
Add it to the work chat where your staff already send receipts, or forward documents to it privately. Staff change nothing and install nothing.
It reads receipts, invoices, PDFs, bank-transfer screenshots and handwritten nota in any language, checks the line items against the total, catches duplicates even when a receipt is re-photographed or sent again by someone else, and writes each document as a row in your own Google Sheet with a link to the original. Every Monday you get a weekly spending report you can forward to a partner.
Setup takes one minute (sign in with Google; the sheet and files stay in your Google Drive). Up to 5 groups per account.
First 40 documents free (20 of them can be your old chat history uploaded as an archive). Then $19/month for 100 documents, $45 for 300, $119 for 1,000.
