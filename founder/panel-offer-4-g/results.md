## What would flip a no

- If the free 30 come back with my real handwritten notas read correctly, totals right and a duplicate actually caught, with almost nothing for me to fix.
- If after the free 30 the Monday report matches what I see in the group chat at night, and my admin tells me herself that she stopped typing receipts.
- If in the free 30 it reads our handwritten nota correctly and flags when a price is out of line with what we normally pay for the same item at the same shop.
- If my builder's staff started paying for materials in cash and sending dozens of receipts a week while I'm abroad, and it caught one real inflated or duplicated invoice during the free trial, the $19 would be easy to justify.
- Running my own staff's worst handwritten nota through the 30 free documents and seeing it get almost all the totals right without me fixing them.
- If it sent the checked receipts straight into Zoho or Jurnal, so it only replaces the part where my bookkeeper digs through Telegram and my main app stays the same.
- If during the free 30 it flags a real duplicate or a padded receipt that I missed myself, I'd pay the $19 without thinking about it.
- Another villa operator I know telling me they've run it for a few months and it caught real duplicates or errors, so I know it works and the company is going to stick around.
- If I could use it without giving it my Google account or adding it to the work group, just forwarding receipts to it myself and getting the sheet back, and another scooter owner I know told me it read their handwritten cash notes correctly.
- If the free 30 caught a real duplicate or a padded parts receipt that my admin missed, money I can actually see I'd lost, I'd pay the $19 without thinking about it.
- If the free 30 documents showed it reading our real handwritten mechanic and parts notas correctly, with almost no fixing, and the weekly sheet came out cleaner and on time compared with my admin's.
- Another builder here in Bali I know telling me he's run it in his work groups for a few months with no problems, and his sheet actually matches what he spent.
