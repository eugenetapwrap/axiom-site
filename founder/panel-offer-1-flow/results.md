## What would flip a no

- If during the free 30 my admin told me herself that it caught up her backlog, got the numbers right, and that she would rather check the bot's sheet than keep typing.
- If it gets my own messy handwritten nota right in the free trial, and it flags prices way above what that supplier normally charges for the same item, not just duplicates.
- If during the free 30 it caught real money I would otherwise have paid twice or missed on the build, more than a few months of the fee, I would keep it for the length of the project.
- If I put 30 of my staff's real handwritten nota through the free trial and almost all of them come out right without me touching them, I'd pay, because then it beats building and babysitting my own bot.
- If it pushed the rows straight into Jurnal or Zoho so my bookkeeper never retypes anything, and I could see a real company behind it that's been running for a while.
- If my accountant takes the sheet as is at month end and stops chasing me for missing receipts, then $19 is nothing next to that hassle and I'd pay.
- Another villa operator in Bali I know telling me they've used it for six months or more without problems, plus a clear name and company behind the bot.
- Someone I know here in Bali running a rental or a shop using it for a few months with no problems, and a way to use it with only receipts I forward myself, without connecting my Google account at all.
- If the free 30 caught a duplicate or an inflated fuel or parts bill my admin missed, so it actually saved me money and didn't just save her typing time.
- If the free 30 showed it reading our handwritten mechanic notes and Indonesian fuel and parts receipts correctly, so my admin really stopped retyping and checking them.
- Another contractor or accountant in Bali I actually know running it with their team for a few months without problems, and showing me the sheet.
- My bookkeeper runs it on one month of our real receipts and tells me she didn't have to chase anyone, or another contractor in Bali tells me he's used it for a year without problems.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
