- If my accountant or another shop owner I know had used it for a few months and told me it was safe, and I could try it by forwarding a few receipts privately without connecting my Google account.
- If the free check found real money lost, like duplicate or padded nota from the building-supply shop worth more than a few months of the fee, I'd pay at least for a month.
