# Board brief: Nota (round 2)

The full product description written by the founder is in `founder/board/product-description.md` — read it in full; it is the main source. Below are the founder's additional statements made in conversation, which take precedence where they are more specific. Nothing here is the chair's opinion.

## Founder's statements in conversation

- **What it does, in the founder's words:** connect the bot to a work chat (or send privately); it collects payment documents by itself. Reads receipts, PDFs, archives, handwritten documents in any format and language. Checks the content inside each document (line items, quantities, prices vs the total) and flags mismatches. Shows that a payment was already submitted — by identical file (hash) and also when the same receipt was re-photographed or sent as a screenshot.
- **Target customer, in the founder's words:** anyone running a business who has a group where employees, the owner and partners drop receipts and payments to be reimbursed or accounted for.
- **Price discussed in conversation:** $19/month for 100 documents, up to 5 groups. Larger packages are open (the description lists $45/300 and $119/1000).
- **Volume reference:** the founder's own company built 3 villas over 8 months and entered about 300 payments in total (~37 a month). The founder believes 100 documents a month is enough for a small business.
- **Live test:** running at one large construction company (the founder's own): about 30 payments a day.
- **Proof that duplicates matter in money:** the same flow, inside Axiom's larger management-accounting system, found a hole of about IDR 1 billion at a development company. The founder says the team knows procurement fraud schemes (duplicate invoices etc.) and that this is confirmed by data.
- **Cost per document:** measured at about $0.01–0.02. Exact duplicate files are caught by hash in code and cost nothing.
- **Hard to copy (founder's claim, not independently verified):** recognition/check modules combine file hashes, LLMs and other (visual) hashing; built and tested over a long time. Note the product description itself says "the bot → recognition → sheet chain can be copied in an evening; the defence is check quality, group rules, local documents, trust and partners — a head start rather than a barrier."
- **Free trial:** onboarding 1 minute; 40 free documents — up to 20 via one archive upload (ZIP / chat export, capped at 20), the other 20 by forwarding or in groups. Founder estimates about 5 days for a normal user to use them.
- **Virality:** visually strong weekly/monthly report cards designed to be forwarded; links to Nota in every touch point (report caption, sheet header, share button, group greeting).
- **Launch plan:** give 10, 20 or 30 free permanent accesses to friends to test; the founder has direct access to about 10 people who actually need it and will ask them to tell friends; then automated posting on Threads.
- **Revenue goal (confirmed by founder after round 2):** 50 clients in month 1, 100 in month 2, 1,000 paying in 12 months (~$30k MRR). The earlier "$10,000/month in 3 months" is withdrawn.
- **Budget:** not stated in money. The description says financing format is undecided (bootstrap from consulting, angel, or strategic partner) and a $300–500 ad test after payments launch.
